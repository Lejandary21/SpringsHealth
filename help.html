❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new patient?

Navigate to the Patient Management tab and click the green "+ Add New Patient" button at the top of the screen.

What does the Red Badge mean?

The red "High Risk" badge indicates a student with a severe medical condition, such as anaphylaxis, that requires an immediate action plan.

How do I update my email address?

Go to the Settings tab, update the email field under Profile Details, and click "Save All Changes" at the bottom.
